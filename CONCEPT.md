# Peakhill NBA Survivor (concept v0.3)

A free, season-long NBA league for Peakhill. Each week you pick three players: one for **points**, one for **rebounds**, one for **assists**. Once you've used a player in a category, you can't use him in that category again this season. You're ranked against everyone in the league, not against one opponent.

It's meant to take about five minutes a week, with no draft, no roster, no waiver wire and no money. You play for bragging rights, and champions get a certificate. Missing a week or joining late shouldn't knock anyone out.

## 1. What's already out there (and what we borrow)

| Game | How it works | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| **Splash Sports NBA Player Survivor** (paid) | Pick a player each day who must clear a stat bar (e.g. 20+ points). Each player can be used once per contest. Run out of options and you're out. | The burn rule and the idea of stat bars. |
| **MLB Beat the Streak** (free) | Pick a batter to get a hit and build a streak. Picks lock 5 minutes before first pitch. "Double Down" lets you pick two batters in one day. | A free, low-effort habit, a one-off power-up, and a lock tied to tip-off. |
| **Golf One-and-Done** | One golfer per tournament, never reused all season. A bad pick never eliminates you. You name an **alternate** who's subbed in if your pick withdraws. | A points table where nobody is eliminated, plus the alternate pick for injuries. |
| **NFL Survivor / office pools** | Pick one team a week and never reuse it. Commissioners add **multiple lives**, **mulligans** and **auto-picks**. | Lives, a one-time comeback, and auto-pick for people who forget. |

## 2. Core rules

**The week.** Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Picks lock at the first tip-off on Monday.

**The picks.** You fill three slots each week:

| Slot | Stat |
|---|---|
| 🏀 Bucket | Points |
| 🧱 Glass | Rebounds |
| 🎯 Dime | Assists |

**What a pick scores: his weekly total.** Your pick scores everything he puts up in that stat from Monday to Sunday. A 50-point night counts in full, and a hot streak keeps adding up. That means the schedule matters: a four-game week beats a two-game week. The pick screen shows each player's **games this week** next to his averages, so that's one more thing to weigh.

**The burn: once per category.** Using a player in a slot burns him **in that slot only**. If Jokić was your Glass pick in week 3, he's gone from Glass, but you can still use him for Dime or Bucket later. Every player can be used up to three times, once in each category. The real question becomes which week to spend him on and in which category. Other people's picks don't affect yours.

**One player, one slot per week.** In any single week, a player can fill only one of your slots.

**Alternates.** Each slot gets an optional backup. If your main pick plays **zero** games that week, the backup scores and is burned in that category instead. Your main pick stays available. If neither one plays, the slot scores 0 and nothing is burned.

---

## 3. Scoring: you vs. everyone

Weekly rebounds and assists run much lower than points (a star's week is about 35 boards against about 110 points), so raw totals can't simply be added together. Instead, each category is ranked across the league every week:

> **In each category, you score 1 point for every colleague whose pick you beat** (½ for a tie).

With 30 players, the top Bucket pick earns 29, the median earns about 15 and the last earns 0. Your weekly score is the sum of your three categories. The season standings add up your weekly scores.

- It's "go against everyone" in the most literal sense.
- Every category is worth the same.
- As the season goes on, everyone runs low on stars together, so late picks still matter.

**Drop your 3 worst weeks.** One drop unlocks after weeks 6, 12 and 18, so by the end only your best 20 of 23 weeks count toward the season title. Vacations, a forgotten Monday or a late start won't sink anyone.

---

## 4. Survivor track: the lives ladder

Your weekly picks also count toward a second title, with no extra work.

- Everyone starts with **3 lives** ❤️❤️❤️.
- The weekly bars are **the same every week**, double weeks included:

  | Bucket | Glass | Dime |
  |---|---|---|
  | **50 points** | **18 rebounds** | **15 assists** |

  These are weekly totals.
- **Clear at least 2 of the 3 bars** to survive the week. Otherwise you lose a life.
- The last person with a life left wins the **Survivor Belt**. If the final players all go out in the same week, the one with the higher season points wins.

The bars don't move, but your options shrink. In October you clear 50 points with Shai in two games. By March, a two-game week from your 20th-best scorer might come up short. Players who lose all their lives **stay in the points table**, so no one stops playing.

## 5. Low-touch design (so more people join)

- **Autopilot.** Miss the deadline, or switch Autopilot on, and the app picks for you in each slot: the unburned player with the highest **projected week** (season average × games this week). You never score a zero, but autopilot spends your best options first, which is a fair cost for not picking.
- **One reminder** goes out on Monday morning with a link straight to the pick screen. That's the only notification.
- **Any time zone.** The lock is shown in local time.
- **Late joiners** start with three lives and play under the drop-3-weeks rule. Monthly titles give them something to win right away.

---

## 6. Titles and certificates

There's no money, just bragging rights and a **downloadable certificate** for every champion. The app generates the certificate (PNG/PDF), signed by the commissioner. It shows the winner's name, the title, the season, and a few lines from their run: final points, best week, and their signature pick (e.g. *"Burned Wemby on Glass in a 4-game week: 61 rebounds"*).

| Title | How it's won | When |
|---|---|---|
| 🏆 **Season Champion** | Most points across the season, after dropping your 3 worst weeks | After week 23 (April) |
| 🥊 **Survivor Belt** | Last person with a life left | Whenever the last one goes out |
| 📅 **Monthly Crown** ×5 | Most points in Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar/Apr | End of each month |
| 🔥 **Playoff Burn Champion** | Winner of the playoff mini-game (below) | After the Finals |

## 7. The fun layer

- **Lock reveal.** When picks lock, everyone's picks become visible with ownership %. "14 of 22 took Jokić for Glass" is good office banter.
- **Weekly awards**, generated automatically:
  - 🧠 **Galaxy Brain**: best result from the least-picked player.
  - 🔥 **Heater**: the biggest single-week total in any category.
  - 🪦 **The Fumble**: a pick who scored 0 (DNP all week, injury).
  - 🧊 **Ice Cold**: the lowest weekly score.
  - 🐐 **Chalk Merchant**: picked the most popular player in all three slots.
- **Two jokers a season:**
  - **Double Down**: one slot earns double rank points this week.
  - **Un-burn**: get back one player in one category, once.
- **Joker cards** (Monopoly Go meets Uno). You earn packs of three cards, keep one, and the other two burn. You can play one card a week. Traps and gifts don't count toward that.
  - **Where packs come from:**
    - a Starter Pack when you join;
    - a Locker Room Pack every week you picked for yourself (not Autopilot);
    - Award Packs for Galaxy Brain, Heater, Ice Cold or the Fumble;
    - a Revenge Pack when you lose a life;
    - an Album Pack for each NBA division you complete in your sticker album. Every player who scores for you adds his team.
  - **Comeback odds:** the top quarter of the standings pulls mostly commons. Only the bottom half can pull Heist and Blue Shell.
  - **The deck (17 cards):**

    | Kind | Cards |
    |---|---|
    | Attacks (blind, before the lock) | Jinx, Load Management, Heist, Blue Shell, Burn Notice |
    | Pranks | Clown Nose, Nickname |
    | Traps, armed face-down | Charge Taken, Uno Reverse |
    | Boosts | Sixth Man, Copycat, Leech |
    | Hindsight, rewriting last week | Switcheroo, Garbage Time |
    | Chaos | Embargo, Scout Report |
    | Head to head | Duel (decline and you wear 🐔) |

  - **Guardrails:**
    - Each person takes at most 2 attacks or pranks a week. Extras fizzle and the card goes back to its owner.
    - Hand limit 4. The oldest card goes first.
    - Cards move rank points but never touch the Survivor Belt: the bars only look at your own picks.
  - **The reveal:** everything is revealed at the lock as a card table, with taunts.
- **Rivals.** Name one coworker as your rival and see your weekly head-to-head record next to the standings.
- **Playoff Burn** (April to June): a separate mini-game with a fresh burn list. Each round you make one pick per category, and the score is that player's total for the round.

## 8. 2026–27 calendar

The regular season runs from **Tue Oct 20, 2026** to **Sun Apr 11, 2027**. Notable dates: NBA Cup group play starts Oct 30, the Cup final is Dec 11, the trade deadline is Feb 11, and All-Star Weekend is Feb 19–21.

| Week | Dates | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tue Oct 20 – Sun Nov 1 | Opening **double week** so people have time to sign up. Same bars. |
| 2–16 | Mon Nov 2 – Sun Feb 14 | Regular weeks |
| 17 | Mon Feb 15 – Sun Feb 28 | All-Star week is light, so it's merged with the next week into a **double week**. Same bars. |
| 18–23 | Mon Mar 1 – Sun Apr 11 | Stretch run, with tanking and resting stars to watch for |

That's **23 picks per category per person**. By March you'll be picking roughly your 20th-best option in each category.

## 10. Build status

Built (see the README for setup):
- **The app:** a static site with an NBA arena look. It covers sign-up and sign-in, picks with backups, a player picker with projections, a live week view, results with the lock reveal, standings, the survivor ladder, a burn book, rivals, trophies and certificates, and rules.
- **Jokers, Joker cards and Rivals:** live from day one. Cards come with pack opening, the reveal, the sticker album, and card effects on the results board.
- **Weekly awards and Monthly Crowns:** calculated automatically.
- **Storage:** Supabase (free tier). Row-level security handles locks and keeps picks hidden until the lock.
- **Hourly GitHub Action:** pulls NBA.com box scores, syncs locks to the real first tip-off, and runs Autopilot.
- **Demo mode** (`/?demo`) for trying it before the season starts.
